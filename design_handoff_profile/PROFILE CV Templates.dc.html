What every template shares
Clear hierarchy, quantified achievement bullets, tabular date alignment, no more than one accent colour, and every field editable in place once wired to a real profile.
ATS note
Templates 1, 3, 4 and 9 are single-column and parse cleanly through applicant tracking systems. Multi-column sidebar templates (2, 6, 10) read best for a human reviewer but risk column-order scrambling in older ATS parsers.
Source of content
Every field here pulls from the same verified-record data used elsewhere in PROFILE — the CV builder never invents an achievement a record doesn't support.
