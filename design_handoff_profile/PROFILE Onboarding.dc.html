STEP 2 OF 5 · ABOUT 30 SECONDS

Confirm it's you

One check now means every record you add afterwards carries weight. Without it, your work sits on PROFILE unverified and recruiters can filter it out.

What happens to the document
It is checked, then deleted. PROFILE stores the result of the check and the date, never the document or its number. Your passport number is not something a recruiter, an employer or PROFILE staff can ever see.
Skipping is fine — you can verify later. Records added before then stay unverified until you do.
STEP 3 OF 5 · THE IMPORTANT ONE

Record one piece of work you finished

Not your whole history. One thing, described plainly. This is what PROFILE is for, and everything else builds on it.

Why this order
Identity comes before verification because a check needs a name to match. Verification comes before the first record so that record can be confirmed immediately. The recommendation comes last because it needs one real record to be honest rather than generic.
Leaving and coming back
Save and finish later exits to Home, where an unfinished onboarding appears as a single card naming the step they stopped on — not a modal, not a nag. Step 2 and step 4 are skippable; steps 1 and 3 are not, because a profile with neither is not a profile.
