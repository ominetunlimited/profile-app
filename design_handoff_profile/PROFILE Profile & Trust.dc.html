What a passport never contains

Rules that hold across every state
A rejected or revoked record is never silently deleted. It stays in the owner's view with the reason, and disappears from public view only.
The verifier's identity is always shown to the record owner. An anonymous verification does not exist on PROFILE.
Expiry is announced 30, 14 and 3 days ahead. A lapsed credential greys out rather than vanishing, so a recruiter sees history rather than a gap.
Every state change is written to an audit trail the owner can read, including who triggered it and when.
